Good automation should make policy easier to follow, not easier to bypass.

When a transaction falls outside an approved budget or lacks required authorization, the system should flag the issue, preserve the evidence, and direct it to a responsible reviewer.

The goal is a faster process with stronger accountability, not an automatic approval for every transaction.
