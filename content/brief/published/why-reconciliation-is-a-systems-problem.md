Reconciliation is often treated as a spreadsheet task. In reality, it is a systems problem involving data quality, business rules, approvals, timing, and accountability.

A useful automation workflow should distinguish a clean match from a transaction that merely looks similar. It should also surface missing documentation and route exceptions to the appropriate reviewer.

The strongest financial automation does more than save clicks: it strengthens controls and makes the process easier to understand and audit.
