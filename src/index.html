Operating principles / 005

Clarity is a competitive edge.

  1. Make the invisible visible. Build traceability into processes, data, and decisions.
  2. Design for trust. Accuracy, documentation, security, and accountability are features.
  3. Build for real people. Technology matters when it improves the work and the outcome.
