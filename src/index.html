EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

Lorenzo Ginyard works at the intersection of finance and technology, building better financial systems through technical capability and business operations knowledge. His experience spans nonprofits, credit unions, pension funds, payroll, and service organizations, with strengths across accounting operations, reconciliations, reporting, audit support, and internal controls.

He is pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science and building practical tools that connect financial insight with technical execution—improving traceability, surfacing exceptions, and helping teams make better decisions from reliable information.

My professional foundation spans accounting operations, payroll, reconciliations, reporting, and internal-audit support. Alongside that experience, I am pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science and building on prior software, data, and cloud learning.

I am interested in the spaces where these disciplines reinforce one another: stronger controls, cleaner data, smarter workflows, and systems that help people make sound decisions.
