THE PROBLEM / 002

Unmatched transactions shouldn't disappear into spreadsheets and inboxes.

Finance teams need a practical way to see what does not match, understand what needs attention, record follow-up, and know which items remain unresolved. ReconIQ is an early-stage workflow concept designed around that need—not a replacement for an accounting system.

WHY THIS PROBLEM / 006

Designed with a finance operator's view of controls and follow-through.

LG Convergence connects financial operations experience with a computer science perspective. A process-improvement project at a community education organization helped move corporate-card receipt collection from roughly 35% to 100% by the end of the audit through a structured tracker and follow-up workflow. That experience informs the emphasis on visibility, ownership, and evidence—not a claim that ReconIQ produced that result.
